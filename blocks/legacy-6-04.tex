
\section{Basic data and properties}
\label{sec:6.4}
The identities below use equipartition, proved in Section~\ref{sec:6.5}.
Put $r'=1-1/r$ and $\tau'=n^2-\tau$. Then
\[
 \pi N=\frac1r u,\qquad \pi(J-N)=r'u,\qquad
 (J-N)\pi^{\mathsf T}=r'u^{\mathsf T},\qquad
 \Omega(J-N)=r'J.
\]
For an idempotent $e$, the $i$th component of $uee^{\mathsf T}$ is the
size of the block containing $i$. Thus $b=uN$ is the expected block-size
row: $b_i$ is the expected size of the partition block containing $i$.
If a kernel partition has block sizes $s_1,\ldots,s_r$, then
\[
 \|uk\|^2=\sum_{a=1}^r s_a^2,\qquad
 \tau=\mathbb E\!\left[\sum_{a=1}^r s_a^2\right]
      =\operatorname{tr}(JN).
\]
In particular,
\[
 \tau'=\operatorname{tr}\bigl(J(J-N)\bigr)
       =\operatorname{tr}\bigl(J(M-\tau\omega)\bigr).
\]
The centered-operator identities are
\[
 u(M-\tau\omega)=\tau'\pi,\qquad
 (M-\tau\omega)u^{\mathsf T}=\tau'\pi^{\mathsf T},\qquad
 J(M-\tau\omega)=\tau'\Omega.
\]
For comparison, the uncentered identities are $uM=n^2\pi$ and
$JM=n^2\Omega$. Finally, for every word $w\in\cS$,
\[
 \pi we=\pi e=\frac1r\rho(e),\qquad
 \pi wN=\frac1r u,\qquad \pi w(J-N)=r'u.
\]
